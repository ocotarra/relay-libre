# Aprende Nostr — Open Curriculum (EN/ES) — Ocotarra
Free for anyone to teach. 6 lessons, 45 minutes each, designed for library workshops.

1. **Why open? / ¿Por qué abierto?** — Platforms can ban, sell data, shut down. Protocols can't.
2. **Keys are identity / Las llaves son tu identidad** — npub = your address (public), nsec = your password (never share). Paper backups.
3. **Relays / Reles** — What a relay is, adding Relay Libre, running a community relay with the Relay Kit.
4. **Your first posts / Tus primeras publicaciones** — Puerta walkthrough, following people, safety settings.
5. **Money without a bank account (intro) / Dinero sin cuenta de banco** — Bitcoin Lightning + zaps at a plain-language level.
6. **Stay safe / Mantente seguro** — Scams, key loss = identity loss, recovery habits, helping family members onboard.

Workshop kit per site: printed workbooks, paper key cards, one loaner laptop, one relay demo box.
Year 1 targets: 500 people onboarded, 10 community relay operators trained, all in New Mexico first.
